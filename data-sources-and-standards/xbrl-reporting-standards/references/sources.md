International baselines: XBRL 2.1, Dimensions 1.0, Inline XBRL 1.1 and the OIM core/JSON/CSV/XML Recommendations. Each still receives errata; read the corrected edition and its date from the index. New supporting documents do not necessarily imply a new major standard, and each — xBRL-CSV Table Constraints, Taxonomy Packages 1.1 — has a status of its own on the index.

The SEC and ESEF observations are dated navigation aids. Verify the relevant reporting program again for each live submission. Examples in this skill are fictional and do not constitute accepted regulator taxonomies or complete filings.
