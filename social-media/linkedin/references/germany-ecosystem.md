# Germany/DACH — Ecosystem

Where the rest of the professional conversation happens, and how it connects to LinkedIn.

## Platforms

| Platform | Role in DACH |
|---|---|
| **XING** | Declining but not gone (~22.5 M users). Still used in traditional Mittelstand recruiting, regionally, and by older professionals. Its "Klartext"/news side is largely irrelevant now |
| **Kununu** | Employer reviews. Owned by New Work SE (XING). **Matters for employer branding** — candidates check it after seeing your LinkedIn |
| **StepStone, Indeed, Arbeitsagentur Jobbörse** | Where jobs are actually found by many candidates. LinkedIn is not the default job channel for most of the German market |
| **Mastodon / Fediverse** | Meaningful German tech and academic presence, especially post-2022 |
| **Bluesky, X** | Fragmented German tech community; X much diminished for professional discourse |
| **Reddit** (r/de, r/Finanzen, r/arbeitsleben, r/informatik) | Honest signal about what people are actually worried about — a good trend-radar source, a bad promotion channel |
| **Discord / Slack communities** | Where practitioners actually talk. Not discoverable, high-value |
| **Newsletters** (Steady, Substack) | Strong German professional-newsletter culture |

## Media and press

Named coverage in these carries weight in a German professional context and is worth linking
from Featured: **Heise / c't / iX** (tech, high credibility), **Golem**, **t3n** (digital
business), **Handelsblatt**, **Wirtschaftswoche**, **Manager Magazin**, **FAZ/SZ** business
sections, **LTO** (legal), and the trade press of the target vertical — which usually matters
more than the general press.

## Events

Conferences are where DACH professional relationships actually form; LinkedIn is the follow-up
layer:

- **re:publica** (Berlin, spring) — digital society
- **OMR** (Hamburg, spring) — marketing and digital business, the largest networking surface
- **Bits & Pretzels** (Munich, autumn) — startups
- **Gamescom** (Cologne, August) — games
- **FOSDEM** (Brussels, February) — open source, heavily German-attended
- **Chaos Communication Congress** (December) and the regional CCC events
- **DMEXCO** (Cologne), **Web Summit Qatar/Lisbon** for the international layer
- Industry-specific trade fairs (Hannover Messe, Automatica, Medica) — for anyone selling into
  German industry these outrank every digital channel

Playbook: post before ("I'll be at X, who else?"), during (one substantive observation, not a
selfie dump), and after (what you learned, who you met — with their permission).

## Institutions worth knowing

- **IHK / HWK** — chambers of commerce and crafts; mandatory membership, useful free advice on
  Gewerbe, tax and legal basics.
- **Bitkom** — the digital industry association; its studies are widely cited and are usable
  source material for posts.
- **Die Medienanstalten** — publish the advertising-labelling guidance referenced in
  `germany-legal.md`.
- **Datenschutzkonferenz (DSK)** and the Landesdatenschutzbehörden — their guidance is what
  German companies actually follow on GDPR questions.
- **BSI** — security guidance; authoritative and citable.

## Funding and startup context

EXIST, INVEST, High-Tech Gründerfonds, KfW programmes, and Land-level funds. Relevant because
"we are hiring / we raised" posts read very differently in a market where public funding is
normal and venture rounds are smaller than in the US.
