# Glossary

**Account override** — the empirically established ranking of formats/hooks on *your* account,
which supersedes published benchmarks. See `content-strategy.md` §4.
**Carousel / Document post** — a PDF posted natively and swiped in-feed.
**Connection degree (1st/2nd/3rd)** — network distance; drives what you can see and message.
**Creator mode** — profile setting making Follow the primary action and surfacing Featured and
newsletters.
**Dwell time** — how long a member spends on a post without necessarily clicking; the
strongest ranking signal, and invisible in your analytics.
**Easy Apply** — one-click application; high volume, low conversion.
**Engagement pod** — coordinated mutual engagement group; detected and penalised.
**Featured** — pinned items on a profile (posts, links, media).
**Fold ("see more")** — the truncation point, ~140 characters on mobile.
**Golden hour / test window** — the first ~60–90 minutes after posting, when signals decide
distribution.
**Impressions vs. members reached** — renders vs. unique people.
**InMail** — paid direct message to non-connections.
**Insight Tag** — LinkedIn's site tracking pixel for conversions and retargeting.
**Lead Gen Form** — on-platform form pre-filled from the member's profile.
**Matched Audiences** — ad targeting from uploaded lists or site retargeting.
**Newsletter** — recurring long-form publication that notifies subscribers.
**Open Profile** — Premium setting allowing messages without an InMail credit.
**Open to Work** — availability signal, recruiter-only or public (green frame).
**Recruiter / Recruiter Lite** — hiring-side product tier.
**Repost vs. repost with thoughts** — bare amplification vs. adding your own commentary; the
first carries little distribution.
**Sales Navigator** — search, lead-list and monitoring tier for sales.
**Social Selling Index (SSI)** — LinkedIn's own 0–100 score; a motivational metric, not a
target worth optimising.
**Suggested / recommended post** — distribution outside your network; the reason reach is not
capped by follower count.
**Thought Leader Ad** — a promoted personal post, run from a company's ad account with the
author's permission.
