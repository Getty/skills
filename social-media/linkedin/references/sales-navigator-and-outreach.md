# Sales Navigator and Outreach

## The tiers

| Tier | Adds |
|---|---|
| Free | Basic search, limited profile views, no InMail |
| Premium Career | Who viewed your profile, applicant insights, InMail credits, LinkedIn Learning |
| Premium Business | More search, company insights, more InMail |
| **Sales Navigator** | Advanced lead/account search, lead and account lists, saved searches with alerts, relationship-explorer, CRM sync, no connection needed to view most profiles |
| **Recruiter / Recruiter Lite** | Candidate pipelines, InMail at scale, hiring-specific filters |

Sales Navigator is a **search and monitoring** tool. Its value is knowing when something
changed (job change, funding, hiring, post activity) — not in sending more messages.

## Search filters worth knowing

Seniority, function, headcount, headcount growth, recent job changes, technologies used,
company follower, posted on LinkedIn in the last 30 days, connections of a specific person.

The single most useful compound filter for B2B: **recently changed job (< 90 days) + target
seniority + target function.** New in role means new budget and new willingness to change
vendors.

## Outreach that does not get ignored

The sequence that works, in order:

1. **Follow and read.** Comment substantively on two or three of their posts over a couple of
   weeks. You are now not a stranger.
2. **Connection request with a note** referencing something specific and real.
3. **After acceptance: do not pitch.** Say why you connected, ask one question you actually
   want the answer to.
4. **Offer something asymmetric** — a relevant finding, an introduction, a piece of your work
   that solves a piece of their problem. Free, no strings.
5. **Only then**, if there is a real fit: name the fit, make an easy no.

The compression of this sequence into a single templated DM is the default behaviour on the
platform and the reason acceptance rates are collapsing. Doing it properly is a competitive
advantage precisely because it is slow.

## InMail

- Subject 200 characters, body 1,900. Credits refresh monthly; unanswered InMails burn credit,
  answered ones return it.
- **Open Profile** members can be messaged without a credit.
- InMail response rates are highest when short (under ~400 characters) and specific.

## Automation — read this before recommending a tool

Third-party automation (Dux-Soup, Phantombuster, Expandi, Linked Helper and similar) violates
LinkedIn's User Agreement. Detection has improved and consequences run from feature
restriction to permanent account loss. Beyond the terms:

- **GDPR**: scraping profile data and importing it into a CRM is processing personal data.
  You need a legal basis, a record of processing, and to honour information duties.
- **UWG (DE)**: unsolicited commercial contact by electronic means is restricted; B2B cold
  outreach on LinkedIn sits in a contested zone and has been litigated. See `germany-legal.md`.

The defensible recommendation is: native tools, saved searches, alerts, and manual
personalisation — plus a CRM on your own side. Say the risk out loud when someone asks for an
automation recommendation; do not just decline.

## Social selling in practice

Content does the qualifying. A prospect who has read three of your posts arrives pre-sold in a
way no sequence achieves. `content-strategy.md` is the sales strategy; this file is the
follow-through.
