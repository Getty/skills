# Ads and Lead Generation

Enough to advise competently and to know when paid is the wrong answer. LinkedIn ads are the
most expensive mainstream social channel per click — they are justified by targeting
precision, not by volume.

## Prerequisites

- A **Company Page** (ads cannot run from a personal profile)
- A **Campaign Manager** account and billing
- The **Insight Tag** on your site for conversion tracking and retargeting — in the EU this
  is a consent-required tracker and creates joint-controllership questions, see
  `germany-legal.md`

## Objectives and formats

| Objective | Formats that fit |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Single image, video, thought-leader ads |
| Consideration (traffic, engagement, video views) | Single image, carousel, video, document ads |
| Conversion (leads, website conversions, job applicants) | **Lead Gen Forms**, conversation ads, document ads |

**Thought Leader Ads** promote an existing *personal* post (with the author's permission).
They typically outperform page-sourced creative for exactly the reason in
`company-pages-and-advocacy.md` — they look like a person, because they are.

**Lead Gen Forms** pre-fill from the member's profile and stay on-platform. Highest conversion
rate of any LinkedIn format; the trade-off is lower lead intent than a landing page.

**Document ads** (gated PDF) are the classic B2B play: the first pages are readable in-feed,
the rest behind the form.

## Targeting

The reason to be here at all: job title, seniority, function, company size, industry, named
company lists, group membership, skills.

- **Matched Audiences**: upload a contact or company list, or retarget site visitors / video
  viewers / form openers.
- **Audience expansion / Audience Network**: off by default for precise B2B — it spends budget
  on adjacency you did not ask for.
- **Audience size**: below ~50,000 delivery becomes unstable; above ~500,000 the targeting
  precision you are paying for is gone.
- **Exclusions matter as much as inclusions** — exclude current customers, current employees,
  competitors, and existing leads.

## Budget reality

- CPC in B2B commonly lands in the several-euro range and higher for senior targeting; CPM
  varies widely. **Do not quote a specific CPC** — it depends on country, seniority and
  competition, and any number you memorised is stale.
- Minimum viable test: enough budget for ~1,000 impressions/day per ad set over two weeks.
  Below that you are buying noise.
- Frequency: B2B audiences are small; the same people see the ad repeatedly. Watch frequency
  and rotate creative before fatigue.

## When paid is the wrong answer

- No organic proof yet. Ads amplify a message; they do not discover one.
- The offer is unclear. Paid traffic to a weak page is a fast way to learn nothing expensively.
- The audience is under a few thousand people — reach them by hand.
- The goal is credibility. Credibility is not purchasable on this platform; posts and
  recommendations build it.

## Measuring

Campaign Manager reports impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions and leads. The number that
matters is **cost per qualified conversation**, which lives in your CRM, not in Campaign
Manager. Wire the two together before spending, or you will optimise CTR and learn nothing.
