## Method, not research

`content-strategy.md`, `archive-and-analysis.md`, the account-override method, the hook
patterns "in practice", the editing passes, the review loops and the outreach sequence come
from operating a real German-language technical account over two years with a full post
archive (impressions, comments, per-format outcomes) — generalised so nothing person-specific
remains. They are engineering judgement and should be calibrated against your own numbers,
which is exactly what `content-strategy.md` §4 tells you to do.

The single strongest observation from that archive, and the one most at odds with published
advice: **on a personal account, story-versus-advertisement dominates format choice.** The same
author, same format, same weekday, saw roughly 150 impressions on a product/audit post and
~20,000 on a build-in-public post that sold nothing. Reach was never capped by follower count.
