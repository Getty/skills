# Company Pages and Employee Advocacy

## The structural fact

**Personal profiles outreach company pages by a wide margin.** Company page posts reach a
fraction of what the same content reaches from a person's profile. This is not a
configuration problem to solve; it is how the platform works. Plan around it.

Consequence: a company page is a **credibility and infrastructure asset**, not a distribution
channel. The distribution comes from people.

## What the page is actually for

- The destination when someone checks whether you are real
- The employer surface for jobs and recruiting
- The target for ads (ads require a page) and the Insight Tag
- The place where product/company announcements live so people can link to them
- Follower base for hiring and product updates

## Setting one up properly

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| Logo 300×300 / cover 1128×191 | Cover is prime unused space — put the value proposition in it |
| Tagline (120 chars) | Appears in search results |
| About | First 2–3 lines visible; same hook discipline as a profile |
| Custom button | Visit website / Contact us / Register |
| Specialties, industry, size, location | Feed search and Sales Navigator filters |
| **Legal notice (Impressum)** | Mandatory in DACH for commercial pages — see `germany-legal.md` |

## Employee advocacy that is not cringe

The instinct — "share our post" — produces bare reshares, which carry almost no distribution
and look coerced. What works:

1. Give employees **material**, not posts to copy: the raw number, the screenshot, the
   customer problem, the engineering decision.
2. Let them write in their own voice, including where they disagree.
3. Never mandate. Mandated advocacy is visible and it devalues everyone's account.
4. Reward the behaviour publicly (internally), not the metric.
5. **Nobody should post the identical text.** LinkedIn detects it and readers notice faster.

## Founder/leader accounts

The highest-return investment for a small company: one or two people posting substantively
from their own accounts, with the company visible in the background. See
`content-strategy.md` §3 for the story-beats-advertising mechanism — it applies doubly when
the author is the founder.

## Page analytics

Page analytics are separate from personal analytics and include follower demographics
(job function, seniority, industry, company size) that personal analytics do not.
That demographic breakdown is the page's genuinely useful output — it tells you who you are
actually reaching, which is worth more than the page's own reach numbers.
