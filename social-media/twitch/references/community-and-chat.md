# Community and Chat

Chat is the product. Everything below is about making it a place people come back to, and
making it survivable when it grows.

## Chat modes and when to use them

| Mode | Effect | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Followers-only (with age requirement) | Only accounts following for ≥ N minutes can chat | **Default for any new or growing channel** — kills drive-by spam |
| Slow mode | N seconds between messages per user | Chat is faster than you can read |
| Subscriber-only | Only subs chat | Raid/hate-raid defence, or a deliberate sub perk |
| Emote-only | Emotes only | De-escalation; also a fun ritual |
| Unique-chat (r9k) | No repeated messages | Copypasta waves |
| Shield Mode | One-click bundle of aggressive restrictions | An attack in progress — see `moderation-and-safety.md` |

## Channel Points

Custom rewards viewers redeem by watching. Since 2026-05 available to **all** streamers, not
just Affiliates. Good rewards give the viewer agency and cost you nothing recurring:
choose the next task, name a thing, pick the music, highlight my message, timeout a friend
(consensually). Bad rewards create obligations you will resent at hour 4.

Channel Point redemptions are available via EventSub for automation (`developer-and-tools.md`).

## Power-Ups and Custom Power-Ups

Bits-funded effects (Gigantify emotes, message effects). Since 2026-05 you can define
**Custom Power-Ups** with your own reward and Bits price. They convert participation into
revenue without feeling like a donation appeal — see `monetization.md`.

## Emotes, badges, Stories

- **Emotes** are the channel's private language and the main reason people subscribe.
  Get them drawn early; they must read at 28 px.
- **Sub badges** mark tenure; **Creator Badge Drops** (2026-05) let you mint event-specific
  badges unlocked by watch-minutes or gifted subs.
- **Stories** — Twitch's between-streams surface; since 2026 the emphasis is clip recaps.
  Post a clip recap after each stream so people who missed you live have an entry point.
- **GIPHY GIFs in chat** for Tier 2/3 subscribers were announced 2026-05-30 with per-channel
  controls (off, frequency, moderation).

## Rituals that build a community

- A **named greeting** for regulars; a **first-message highlight** for newcomers.
- A recurring segment with a fixed time slot ("Friday: your code, reviewed").
- A **Discord** with one channel that is *not* about the stream.
- A **raid tradition** — always raid, always say who you are raiding and why.
- Watch Streaks and sub anniversaries acknowledged out loud.

## Moderators

Recruit from regulars, not from strangers who volunteer. Give them: written rules, explicit
authority ("you do not need to ask me"), an escalation path, and a private channel. Twitch
shows **mod anniversaries** in chat (from 2026-06) — use them.

Mods are also your compliance layer in DACH: chat is personal data and your legal
responsibility (see `germany-legal.md` for the data-protection notes).

## Guests and co-streams

**Stream Together / Guest Star** brings up to 5 guests into the stream from desktop or mobile
with no third-party software. Two things to settle before going live with a guest: what
happens if they say something that violates policy (you are responsible for your channel),
and whether the guest's audio is on a separate track for the VOD edit.
