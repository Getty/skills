# Germany/DACH — Advertising, Sponsorship and Gambling

**Not legal advice.** Verified 2026-08.

## 1. Ad labelling — the rule everyone gets wrong

Two legal layers apply at once:

- **§ 22 MStV** — advertising must be **clearly recognisable and clearly separated** from
  editorial content; surreptitious advertising (Schleichwerbung) is prohibited.
- **§ 5a Abs. 4 UWG** — failing to disclose commercial intent is an unfair commercial practice.
- **§ 6 DDG** — commercial communications must be identifiable.

**When you must label** (settled by the BGH's influencer judgments of 2021-09-09 and
2022-03, and the Medienanstalten's *Leitfaden Werbekennzeichnung bei Online-Medien*):

| Situation | Label required? |
|---|---|
| Paid sponsorship, brand deal, Creator Sponsorship, Bounty Board campaign | **Yes** |
| Free product received in exchange for coverage ("Gratis-Produkt") | **Yes** — BGH 2022 confirmed a gift is consideration |
| Affiliate link / referral code (even unpaid until conversion) | **Yes** |
| Twitch Drops campaign run for a publisher | **Yes**, treat as sponsored |
| Your own product/merch/Patreon | Generally yes as commercial communication — but self-promotion by a commercial channel is usually obvious; label anyway, it is free |
| Genuinely unpaid, self-bought product you simply like, no link, no consideration | **No** — BGH 2021: not every product mention is advertising |

**How to label on a livestream:**
- Say it **out loud at the start of the segment** and repeat it when new viewers arrive — the
  legal test is whether the *average viewer* recognises it, and viewers join mid-stream.
- Put it **on screen** for the duration of the sponsored segment ("Werbung" / "Anzeige").
- Put it in the **stream title** where the whole stream is sponsored, and in panels.
- German terms are the safe ones: **"Werbung"**, **"Anzeige"**, **"Dauerwerbesendung"**.
  English "ad"/"sponsored", hashtags buried at the end, or "in friendly cooperation" are not
  reliably sufficient for a German audience.
- A segment where advertising is a substantial part and which runs **longer than 90 seconds**
  is treated as a **Dauerwerbesendung** and must be labelled as such throughout
  (MA HSH guidance).

Enforcement comes from the Landesmedienanstalten and from competitors/Wettbewerbszentrale via
Abmahnung — the second is the expensive one.

## 2. Gambling — much stricter than Twitch's own policy

Two separate layers, and the German one is stricter:

**Twitch's policy** (since 2022-10-18) prohibits streaming certain slots, roulette and dice
sites that are not licensed in the US or another jurisdiction with sufficient consumer
protection.

**German law (GlüStV 2021)** goes further:

- Public gambling may only be offered or **advertised** with a **German licence**. Promoting
  an unlicensed online casino is prohibited — and courts have applied this to streamers.
- **§ 5 Abs. 3 GlüStV 2021**: advertising for virtual slot machines, online poker and online
  casino games is **prohibited on broadcast and internet between 06:00 and 21:00**.
- Advertising directed at minors, or using prominent figures appealing to minors, is
  prohibited.
- **OVG Sachsen-Anhalt (2024-08)** upheld a prohibition order against a streamer for streaming
  their own participation in gambling that is unlawful in Germany. **A stream showing you
  gamble is treated as advertising for that offer** — the "I'm not advertising, I'm just
  playing" defence has failed.
- Living abroad does not help if the stream targets Germany (Werbung für illegales
  Glücksspiel, decided 2024).

**Practical rule for an agent:** if a German-facing streamer asks about casino/slots content,
the answer is that this is a licensed-activity minefield with a 06:00–21:00 blackout and
active enforcement, and they need specialist legal advice before, not after.

## 3. Alcohol, tobacco, medicines, finance

- Alcohol advertising: no targeting of minors, no encouragement of excessive consumption
  (§ 22 MStV, Lebensmittelrecht).
- Tobacco/e-cigarettes: advertising largely prohibited (TabakerzG).
- Health claims and medicines: HWG rules, very restrictive.
- **Financial/crypto promotion**: securities and crypto promotion carries its own disclosure
  duties (WpHG, MiCAR since 2024/2025). "Not financial advice" in chat is not a shield.

## 4. Austria and Switzerland

- **Austria**: labelling under UWG (AT) and MedienG; the same "recognisable as advertising"
  principle. Gambling is a state monopoly area (GSpG) with strict advertising limits.
- **Switzerland**: UWG (CH) plus the Lauterkeitskommission's rules; gambling advertising
  restricted under BGS. Terms in German are equally expected.
