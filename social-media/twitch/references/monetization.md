# Monetization

**This page changed fundamentally in May–June 2026.** Any guide written before that is wrong
about who can earn what. All figures verified 2026-08 against Twitch's own announcements
(`sources.md`); re-verify anything you are about to quote to a streamer.

## The 2026 reset: "Monetization for All"

Since **2026-05-13** (US first, then globally over roughly a week), **Bits, subscriptions,
emotes, badges and Channel Points are available to all eligible streamers**, not only
Affiliates and Partners. Onboarding runs through the Creator Dashboard.

Source: Twitch blog, *Monetization for All*, 2026-05-13.

What Affiliate/Partner status still decides: **getting paid out**. Every streamer can have
the tools; only Affiliates and Partners receive a payout. Until then, earnings sit in
**Spendable Balance** — usable inside Twitch (buying Bits, gifting subs), unlockable below
the $50 minimum payout threshold, and automatically paid out once you become eligible and
cross $50. Spendable Balance launched in the US and is rolling out globally through 2026.

> If you are advising someone who "can't monetize until Affiliate", that advice is out of
> date. Correct it explicitly — it changes what a new streamer should do in week one.

## Affiliate requirements (updated 2026-06)

| Old requirement | Current requirement (as of 2026-06) |
|---|---|
| Stream 8 hours | **Stream 4 hours** |
| Stream on 7 different days | **Stream on 4 different days** |
| Average 3 CCV | **Min 3 ACCV (on 4 different days)** |
| 50+ followers | **25+ followers** |

Measured over a rolling 30-day window. Qualifying streamers get an email and a Creator
Dashboard notification to start onboarding. Source: Twitch blog, *Monetization for All*.

## Partner

Partner remains an application-based status (Creator Dashboard → Achievements → Path to
Partner), judged on consistency, concurrent viewership, schedule and content quality rather
than one published number. Under Monetization for All, the *tools* that used to define
Partner are no longer exclusive to it; what Partner still brings is priority support, more
emote/badge slots, and eligibility for programs and promotions.

## Revenue splits — the Plus Program

The historical "70/30 for big streamers, 50/50 for everyone else" model is now the
**Plus Program**, points-based and open to qualifying Affiliates *and* Partners:

| Level | Split (streamer/Twitch) | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 50/50 net | default |
| Plus Level 1 | **60/40** | ≥100 Plus Points for 3 consecutive months |
| Plus Level 2 | **70/30** | ≥300 Plus Points for 3 consecutive months |

Plus Points are earned from paid subscriptions, with higher tiers weighted more than Tier 1.
The 70/30 rate applies up to a revenue cap (historically US$100k of subscription revenue per
12 months); above the cap the rate returns to 50/50.

Reported consistently across trade coverage 2025-09 to 2026-07 (influencermarketinghub,
streamscharts, kudos.tv, socialday). **Twitch's own help pages are the authority** — treat
these numbers as directional and verify in the Creator Dashboard before a streamer plans
around them. Legacy contracts: creators previously on guaranteed 70/30 deals were migrated
into the Plus Program (reported 2026-02); some were told no action was needed to keep 70/30.

## Revenue streams, ranked by how much they actually matter

1. **Subscriptions** (Tier 1/2/3, gifted subs, Prime sub). Recurring, predictable, the base
   of any real income. Gift-sub events are the spikiest source: streamer-led promos (35 %
   discount on gift-sub bundles, **96 promotional hours/year** as of 2026-05) drive up to
   **80 % more gift subs** during the promo (Twitch, 2026-05-30).
2. **Bits / Cheers**, including **Power-Ups** (viewers spend Bits on effects — Gigantify an
   emote, chat effects) and, since 2026-05, **Custom Power-Ups** you define yourself
   (e.g. "chat picks my next move" for 1,000 Bits).
3. **Hype Trains** — **more than a third of viewer spending** goes through Hype Trains
   (Twitch, 2026-05-19). Special variants exist: Community Trains reward non-spenders too and
   earn **2× a standard Hype Train**; Mythic Trains grant a 24-hour golden emote. They fire
   randomly on a small share of trains.
4. **Ads** — mid-roll ads via the Ads Manager / Ads Incentive Program. Revenue per hour is
   low relative to subs; the real cost is viewer churn at the ad break. Pre-rolls are reduced
   for channels that run their own mid-rolls.
5. **Creator Sponsorships** — brand deals brokered by Twitch, explicitly being opened to
   smaller channels (3× the participating streamers in 2026 vs 2025). **Bounty Board is not
   dead**: it has been folded into Creator Sponsorships as *Open Invite campaigns*, now
   available to **Affiliates as well as Partners** (Twitch, 2026-05-30). A Minecraft campaign
   ran with 6,000+ streamers, over half of them Affiliates.
6. **Drops** — viewers claim in-game items for watching. 36 M+ viewers claimed a Drop in the
   past year; sub-gated Drops put an additional **$11 M** into streamers' pockets (Twitch,
   2026-05-30).
7. **Creator Badge Drops** (2026-05) — you design a custom chat badge for an event, viewers
   unlock it by watch-minutes or gifted subs. Testing showed up to **50 % more gift-sub
   revenue** on day one of an event.
8. **Direct donations** (PayPal, StreamElements, Streamlabs) — bypass Twitch's cut entirely,
   and in Germany are **taxable income**, not gifts, in the normal case. See
   `germany-taxes-and-business.md`.
9. **Off-platform**: Patreon/Steady, merch, YouTube ad revenue on repurposed VODs, affiliate
   links (which trigger labelling duties, see `germany-advertising-and-gambling.md`).

## Payout mechanics

- Minimum payout threshold: **US$50**.
- Payments run on a delay (typically ~15 days after month end, when the threshold is met).
- **Eurozone streamers**: from summer 2026 Twitch removes currency-conversion fees on SEPA
  payouts (previously 1–2.5 % lost per payout) (Twitch, 2026-05-30).
- Tax forms: non-US streamers complete a **W-8BEN** (individual) or W-8BEN-E (company) in the
  Twitch tax interview. Without it, US withholding is applied at the maximum rate. With the
  DE–US double-taxation treaty properly claimed, withholding on most streaming income is
  reduced or eliminated — see `germany-taxes-and-business.md`.

## What to tell a streamer who wants to "make money on Twitch"

Order of operations that matches how the money actually behaves:

1. Turn on the tools now (they are no longer gated) and get to Affiliate — it is four hours
   over four days with 25 followers, which is weeks, not years.
2. Build **recurring** revenue (subs) before chasing one-off spikes.
3. Use the participation features (Hype Train, Power-Ups, badge drops, gift-sub promos) —
   they are where a third or more of spending happens, and they are free to set up.
4. Treat sponsorships as a consequence of an engaged community, not a substitute for one.
5. Sort the tax and business side **before** the first payout, not after (DACH: Gewerbe,
   Kleinunternehmer status, W-8BEN).
