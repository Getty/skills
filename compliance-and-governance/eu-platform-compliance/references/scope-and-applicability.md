# Scope and applicability

## Start with an entity and service map

Map each legal entity and its specific service. A German company selling AI access does not acquire every role named in digital legislation. An AI API customer may be a controller for its employees, while the platform is a processor for that workload and a controller for its own billing or abuse prevention. AI Act provider/deployer and GDPR controller/processor are different classifications. An infrastructure tenant does not automatically become a cloud-computing provider under NIS2. A software brand does not alone decide copyright or product-manufacturer responsibility.

Record intended and reasonably foreseeable use, marketing claims, contractual allocation, technical control and actual behaviour. Do not choose whichever label creates the fewest obligations.

## Initial screen

| Regime | Trigger to investigate | Fact needed | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GDPR | Processing personal data within territorial scope | Data/individuals/purposes, establishments and targeting | Business data or pseudonyms are nonpersonal |
| National ePrivacy | Terminal access/storage and communications marketing | Client behaviour, service requested, recipient/channel/country | Server-side analytics never needs assessment |
| AI Act | Placing/providing/using systems or models in scope | Entity role, intended purpose, model/system version, launch date | Every LLM wrapper is a GPAI model provider or high-risk system |
| Consumer acquis | Transactions with natural persons outside their trade | Contract purpose, targeting, product type, consideration | A business checkbox removes consumer rights |
| EAA/national accessibility | Covered consumer products/services | Service category, national law, entity size and exclusions | Every website is covered identically; every SME is exempt |
| DSA | Intermediary activity, hosting or platform function | User-provided information, storage, public dissemination | All AI output is hosted third-party information |
| NIS2/national cyber law | Covered entity/sector/size or special category | Service actually offered, group size, establishment, designation | All SaaS is in scope, or a small company can never be in scope |
| CRA | Making products with digital elements available in the course of a commercial activity | Delivered software/hardware, remote processing dependency, responsible role | Pure SaaS and distributed software have identical treatment |
| Data Act | Data processing services, connected products or other relevant chapter | Definitions, service type, customer contract, exemptions | Every export request is GDPR portability |
| IP and trade secrets | Ingesting, copying, transforming, training or publishing | Rights chain, access, permission, purpose and provenance | Public web access or an API licence clears all rights |
| VAT/accounting/payments | Supply, invoicing, records, handling funds | Seller/merchant of record, customer/tax status, locations, supply type | Germany-only VAT or a payment SDK handles all duties |

Use the legal anchor catalogue in sources.md and the focused skill for exact tests. The table is a screen, not a legal determination.

## An applicability record must be falsifiable

Record an identifier, feature, entity, countries, legal role, provision/version, reasoning, facts with evidence, exceptions examined, responsible reviewer, checked date and reopening event. For an exemption, capture its conditions and duration. Example: an accessibility services microenterprise assessment needs relevant headcount and financial criteria, not simply the word 'startup'. Separate entity/group calculations required by different regimes.

Write `unknown` when turnover, processing purpose, public dissemination or target sector has not been established. Continue unaffected development while obtaining that fact; contain the specific affected feature if its release relies on the unresolved conclusion.

## Resolve collisions

Retention for tax evidence does not authorise retaining raw conversations for the same period. Auditability does not require logging every prompt. A customer instruction cannot make prohibited AI lawful. A Data Act switching obligation does not override third-party rights in software or another tenant's data. A contractual promise can exceed a statutory minimum and still bind the platform.

Document the competing requirements, their precise records/features, access restrictions, legal-hold boundary and decision owner. Prefer separable data classes and scoped controls over a single global retention period.

## Deliverable

Produce one obligation register with links into specialist decisions. Do not create disconnected reports whose assumptions disagree. Preserve rejected options and unresolved facts without copying sensitive source material into an unrestricted repository.
