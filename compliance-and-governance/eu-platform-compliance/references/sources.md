# Source hierarchy and legal anchors

Research snapshot: 2026-10-22. Sources are current-source entry points, not permanent legal guarantees. Recheck amended/consolidated versions and national implementation for the actual decision date.

## Hierarchy

Prefer Official Journal/authenticated gazette text, current consolidated law, relevant binding court decisions, official regulatory guidance, recognised technical standards, and only then commentary for discovery. A consolidated text is useful but the official published acts control. A news headline or political agreement does not amend a statute. A code of practice or harmonised standard can have a specific legal role; determine that role instead of treating every 'best practice' as mandatory.

## Retrieval limits

During preparation, several EUR-Lex direct document opens returned anti-bot pages, and some German-law opens timed out. Accessible official article reproductions, official indexed statute excerpts and regulator publications were used where available. Do not label an inaccessible original as fully read. Source-level registries and the AI Act transition file identify the operative alternatives. For an uncertain decisive provision, obtain the authoritative text before making a high-impact legal conclusion.

## Evidence discipline

For a new claim, retain URL, issuer, document/version, precise provision, publication/effective/application dates, retrieved date, access mode and any limitations. Separate the text of the rule from your inference about the platform. Mark summaries and unresolved interpretation explicitly. Do not copy whole sources into the platform repository unless necessary and permitted.
