# Country and specialist-service routing

## Keep the European core portable

An English skill improves reuse; it does not harmonize national law. Record EU/EEA/other territory, establishment, customer targeting, language, audience and specific service. A Germany-based company can still face country-specific mandatory consumer, content, professional or employment rules. Apply the relevant EU conflict/country-of-origin framework before assuming every target country's entire law applies.

Use a national adapter with these fields:

`country | service/audience | establishment/targeting facts | national rule | primary source/version | EU interaction | applicability date | regulator/contact role | implementation change | reviewer`

Never clone Germany's Impressum wording, GDPR child-consent age, youth-protection-officer test, court-request route, media rules or employment co-determination into another country without checking its law. UK and Switzerland are separate legal reviews; EEA relevance in an EU act is not proof of completed EEA incorporation.

## New service screens

| Activity | Required focused assessment | Facts to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Medical/therapy diagnosis or treatment | Medical-device/professional rules, health data, AI Act | Intended purpose and claims, decisions, professional involvement, users |
| Personalized legal advice | National legal-service authorization and professional secrecy | Advice versus general information, audience, lawyer involvement |
| Investment/credit/insurance/payment actions | Financial licensing, consumer/AI rules, payments and DORA customer requirements | Money/decision flow, custody, authority, customer role |
| Recruitment/worker ranking | AI Act high-risk/prohibited uses, labor/discrimination/privacy law | Effect on hiring/work, human decision, attributes, worker notice |
| School/student assessment | AI Act education classification, children, public-sector/national education rules | Purpose, age, authority, special data, assessment effect |
| Public administration/law enforcement | Specific legal authority, procedural safeguards, AI/GDPR or law-enforcement data regime | Public power involved, affected rights, legal basis |
| Political advertising or election service | Relevant EU political-advertising and national electoral law | Paid sponsor, targeting, publication, campaign role |
| Gambling/adult audiovisual content | Licenses, age/access, national media rules | Content category, target country, payment, user age |
| Telecom or private communications | Sector confidentiality, metadata, security and lawful requests | Actual interpersonal communication function and provider role |
| Digital labor intermediation | Employment classification and platform-work rules/implementation | Control over tasks, algorithmic management, workers, country |

These are scope triggers, not ready-made legal conclusions. Retrieve current primary instruments and national implementation before turning a trigger into a mandatory control. Route ordinary security, privacy and consumer duties to their existing suite skills while the sector review proceeds.

## Internal compliance agents can themselves create duties

Monitoring source code and infrastructure is different from monitoring employees' private communications or measuring their performance. Define the agent's purpose, permitted evidence sources, access, retention, recipients and consequences. Prefer aggregate technical control evidence over individual productivity or sentiment scoring where that serves the purpose.

For German workers, screen BDSG/GDPR employment rules and BetrVG §87(1)(6) co-determination when technical tools can monitor behavior/performance, using current legal interpretation and the actual works-council situation. Source: CON-WORKS for the statutory anchor; specialized employment-law review is required for implementation. Do not infer consent from an employee having a business account or from the employer owning the device.

The compliance agent must not quietly add emotion inference, behavioral profiling, covert communications access or employment decisions under the label “regulatory monitoring”. Route AI Act prohibited/high-risk classification and labor/privacy analysis first. Keep technical assurance permissions narrow and audit the agent's own access.

## Specialist review handoff

Provide a concrete feature description, intended/foreseeable use, users/countries/ages, data sources, decisions/actions, contractual roles, marketing claims, deployment, current controls, primary-source questions and proposed scope restriction. Request resolution of the specific issue rather than an undifferentiated “legal approval”. Continue reversible implementation work that does not prejudge that decision.
