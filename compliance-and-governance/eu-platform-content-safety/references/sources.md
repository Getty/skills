## Source discipline

Store article/paragraph, legal version, checked date, applicable date, country, scope facts, status, and reviewer. Some official sites blocked full retrieval; use an official alternative or retrieve the OJ/national gazette before a detailed operational conclusion. A search snippet can locate a rule but is not enough for a disputed exception. Do not reproduce statutory text wholesale; keep concise original operational explanations and links.

Check fresh sources for P2B repeal, AI Act changes, youth rules and designation decisions, e-evidence national implementation, DSA templates, and regulator contacts. Distinguish proposals such as future child-abuse regulation from enacted reporting/removal duties. Never use an outdated press article to introduce an unverified general message-scanning obligation.
