# Marketplaces, agents/apps, ranking and business users

## Trigger conditions

Reopen the legal architecture when users can sell models, agents, prompts, datasets, consulting, subscriptions, apps or physical goods through the platform. Identify the actual seller, buyer type, contracting parties, payment flow, who sets price, ranking and promotion, who provides the service, and who bears support/refund responsibilities.

Selling the operator's own AI subscription is not automatically operating a third-party marketplace. Conversely, calling third-party sellers “partners” does not remove marketplace duties. A store can serve business sellers and consumer buyers simultaneously.

## DSA marketplace layer

For online platforms allowing consumers to conclude distance contracts with traders, assess Articles 30–32 and the Article 29 exemption. Collect/verify the required trader information with the statutory best-efforts/reliability process, display required trader information, design interfaces to allow mandatory product/service information, and handle identified illegal products/services and affected customers as required. Do not collect identity documents for every ordinary chatbot user merely because trader traceability exists.

Store trader verification separately from public profiles. Publish only what the law requires; identification documents and payment details are not automatically public. Restrict access and retention, and document the basis. Apply data-protection security to seller onboarding. Source: CON-DSA.

## P2B scope and change watch

Regulation 2019/1150 applies conditionally to online intermediation and search relationships, including relevant EU business users offering goods/services to consumers in the Union. A private business-use AI API is not automatically an online intermediation service. Assess the definition, contractual relationship, consumer transaction facilitation and territory rather than the B2B label.

The Commission's current policy page identifies a proposal to repeal P2B announced on 2025-11-19. Treat that as a proposed change unless an enacted repeal with an operative date is verified. The 2026 AI Act amendment is not itself evidence that P2B has disappeared. Sources: CON-P2B and CON-P2B-STATUS.

For an in-scope relationship, assess terms availability and changes, restriction/suspension/termination reasons and notice, ranking parameters, ancillary goods/services, differentiated treatment, access to data, restrictions on off-platform offers, and complaints/mediation, including article-specific small-enterprise exemptions. Do not remove all business-user protections on a generic SME assumption.

## Ranking and advertising

Inventory organic ranking, recommendations, sponsored placement, affiliate links, paid boosts, operator-preferred agents and model-provider commercial arrangements. Explain relevant main ranking parameters and their relative importance where the applicable rule requires it; a disclosure obligation is not a blanket duty to reveal source code or exploitable signals.

Separate ad placement from genuine relevance results. Make sponsor/payer/beneficiary and targeting information available according to the applicable DSA/consumer rules. Assess sensitive-category profiling and minors' ads separately. Do not allow a monetization flag to silently change a user's safety/quality ranking without the required transparency. Route direct marketing and tracking to their dedicated skill.

## Other regimes to screen

- Consumer law: seller identity/status, contract formation, price, digital-content/service rights, withdrawal and remedies; use consumer-commerce.
- Payments: custody, wallets, stored value and merchant-of-record structure may need payment-regulation review; using a PSP does not answer every role question.
- VAT/platform reporting: digital sales, deemed-supplier rules, DAC7 activity/operator scope and seller reporting need tax assessment; do not assume an agent store is automatically reportable or exempt.
- Product safety: physical-goods marketplaces require review of the General Product Safety Regulation and product-sector law; a software-only platform is not automatically within every physical-product duty.
- Copyright/licensing: uploaded models/datasets/prompts/software have different rights; seller warranty alone may be insufficient after a substantiated notice.
- Competition and DMA: assess actual designation and conduct; ordinary platforms are not automatically gatekeepers.

These are routing triggers requiring current primary-law research before a conclusion, not assertions that all regimes apply. Sources for the core layer are CON-DSA and CON-P2B.

## Tests and records

Test a private seller falsely claiming business status, missing required trader fields, suspended trader appeals, a sponsored result mistaken for organic, a seller's changed identity, a substantiated illegal listing, notices to affected purchasers, and seller data export/deletion subject to retention. Preserve seller-role decisions, contracts, verification outcomes, ranking-version evidence, notice history and corrective action. Do not test with real forged documents or initiate live payments without authorization.
