# Government requests, urgent content orders and e-evidence

## Preserve independent routes

Separate DSA Articles 9/10 orders, DSA Article 18 suspected-crime notifications, TCO removal orders, e-evidence production/preservation orders, ordinary national criminal-process requests, and civil/IP orders. A legal instrument's scope, validity, addressee, jurisdiction, data categories, timing, secrecy, remedies and transmission route differ.

Sources: CON-DSA, CON-ART18, CON-ART18-GUIDE, CON-TCO, CON-TERR, CON-EEVIDENCE, CON-EEVIDENCE-DIR, CON-EBEWMG.

## Intake and authenticity

Record exact receipt time, timezone, channel, sender, claimed authority, certificate/order ID, legal basis, jurisdiction, object/data scope, urgency, deadline and secrecy instructions. Independently verify official authority and channel; do not trust a logo, urgent subject or model interpretation. Do not upload private content or credentials to a URL supplied in an unverified request.

Use the assigned legal/incident contact and deputy. Keep preserved evidence and draft responses access-controlled. Compare the request with the service's actual role and the relevant legal mechanism. A narrow order must not become an unrestricted export. Identify third-party rights, privilege, conflict of laws, inability to comply, or grounds to object without silently stopping the clock.

## DSA orders and Article 18

Articles 9 and 10 set conditions concerning orders against illegal content and orders to provide specified recipient information. Examine issuing authority, legal basis, reasoning, exact locations/identifiers, territorial scope, redress and notification requirements. Inform the relevant authority about effect given to the order as required. Do not invent a universal DSA 24-hour order deadline; the actual order and legal instrument matter.

Article 18 requires hosting providers that become aware of information giving rise to suspicion of a criminal offence involving a threat to a person's life or safety to inform the relevant law enforcement/judicial authorities promptly. It is not an instruction to report every terms violation or monitor every private message. Use the current official good practices adopted 2026-09-25 and contact-point list. Determine the concerned Member State by the statutory links; where it cannot be identified with reasonable certainty, follow the establishment/representation/Europol rule.

Do not copy a permanent list of email addresses into a skill. Verify the current destination before any authorized submission. Prepare the facts, lawful disclosure scope and urgency for the responsible person. If the agent lacks authority to contact third parties, expose that specific immediate decision and deadline to the user; do not silently send a report.

## Terrorist Content Online Regulation

Regulation 2021/784 applies to the relevant hosting services disseminating information to the public in the Union. A private store is not automatically within this public-dissemination definition. Evaluate statutory exclusions/context, including educational, journalistic, artistic or research material as applicable. Source: CON-TCO and CON-TCO-GUIDE.

A valid Article 3 removal order generally requires removal or disabled access in all Member States within one hour of receipt. This is distinct from a voluntary deletion request or a routine DSA notice. Implement an actually staffed route, timestamped intake, precise item resolution and controlled enforcement. Use statutory response procedures for force majeure, factual/legal impossibility or insufficient information; do not treat technical difficulty as automatic suspension.

Record review/remedy rights, content-provider information and required preservation. TCO Article 6 has specific preservation rules for removed content and related data, generally six months with lawful extensions; route retention to a restricted legal-hold process. Do not erase evidence immediately or keep it broadly available. In Germany, TerrOIBG allocates roles including BKA orders and BNetzA supervision; verify the exact action and authority.

## E-evidence, effective from 2026-08-18

Regulation 2023/1543 creates European Production Orders and European Preservation Orders for covered providers. Directive 2023/1544 addresses designated establishments/legal representatives. Scope is wider than classic telecom: inspect actual covered information-society/storage/domain services, EU offering, cross-border circumstances and participation rules. Do not assume every website qualifies or every Member State participates identically.

Check the German EBewMG (promulgated in BGBl I 2026 No64) and current BfJ information for designated addressees, notification, powers and provision-specific commencement. The Federal Government's August 2026 overview is useful status information but does not replace the statute's individual commencement clauses. Source: CON-EBEWMG, CON-EEVIDENCE-GUIDE.

For a production certificate, inspect Articles 10–13 and applicable safeguards: ordinary production can be due within ten days, with eight-hour emergency handling under the specified conditions. For preservation, secure the identified existing data without undue delay and apply the Article 11 retention/extension/termination process. Preserve first where lawfully required; do not confuse preservation with permission to disclose. Check any notification to the enforcing authority, suspensive effects, objections, extension/follow-up and confidentiality before transmitting data.

These are not general data-retention mandates and do not authorize building a new surveillance feed. Return only data within the validated order and actually within possession/control as the law requires; accurately report technical impossibility or missing data using the legal mechanism. Do not fabricate a response or decrypt data by inventing a key the provider does not possess.

## Operational acceptance

Tabletop a valid urgent order, a forged email, an incorrect tenant, a preservation-only order, a content order covering one URL, cross-border uncertainty and an unavailable primary contact. Use mock destinations and synthetic data. Show timely qualification, authorized execution, review/objection route, restricted preservation, delivery receipts and follow-up. Never file test notices in a live authority system.

After each case retain a decision log, legal source version, deadline calculations, exact data/item scope, approval/delegation, actual effect/submission, acknowledgement, secrecy/user-notification handling and hold expiry. A completed draft is not a completed disclosure or filing.
