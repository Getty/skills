# Change impact workflow

## Build a precise delta

1. Record the old and new provision/version/status.
2. Identify what changed: scope, entity role, threshold, conduct, interface, evidence, authority, deadline, transition or sanction.
3. Identify the affected platform features, customers, countries, vendors and existing decisions.
4. Decide applicability with reasons and unresolved facts.
5. Translate the requirement into product/configuration/process changes and meaningful acceptance evidence.
6. Assign an accountable owner, internal target and legally grounded deadline where relevant.
7. Update the obligation register, relevant skill/reference, test expectations and release record together.

Do not edit every document just because a source page changed. Link a single authoritative decision to affected controls and preserve version history.

## Example: new consumer interface right

If legislation introduces an online withdrawal function, identify the contract types and interface conditions, exact applicability date, availability period and required confirmation. Review the actual signup/subscription state model, make withdrawal distinct from contractual cancellation, update accessible UI and confirmations, and test legally relevant states. Do not simply add a button that sends an untracked email and claim completion.

## Example: AI timing amendment

Determine which provisions and cohorts move, which remain applicable, and whether existing-system or model transitions differ. Re-plan future high-risk documentation work without disabling already applicable transparency, prohibition or privacy controls. Update the source registry and classifier together, not just a deadline spreadsheet.

## Example: vendor terms change

Identify the contractual product, effective date, customer override and affected data/model routes. Check whether new retention, training, support access or licence conditions conflict with platform promises. Prepare a configuration change, alternative provider, notice or agreement update under the relevant authority. Do not accept new terms or notify customers automatically.

## Completion

Close a change item only when its scope decision is recorded and all required tasks/evidence have a disposition. `no_platform_effect` needs a reason and reopening condition. A policy update without matching implementation is not closure. If a legal date is missed, report the actual gap and remedial plan; do not rewrite history or mark the old version as compliant.
