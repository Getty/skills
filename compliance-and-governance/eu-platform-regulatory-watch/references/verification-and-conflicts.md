# Verification and source conflicts

## Retrieval record

Record URL, issuer, identifier, title/version, provision, language, retrieved time, source type and access method. Distinguish full text, official reproduction, indexed official text, official summary, limited access and unavailable. A search excerpt can be useful but is not evidence that the entire act was inspected.

Several EUR-Lex direct pages returned anti-bot responses in this collection's research; some German-law pages and ministry guidance intermittently timed out. Do not treat a failed request as repeal, inexistence or permission to rely on memory. Use a permitted official alternative (gazette, official reproduction, authoritative published PDF) and disclose the limit.

Do not bypass access controls or fabricate a document. If the decisive text remains unavailable, state the narrow verification gap and obtain it through an authorised source. Continue unaffected work and preserve existing applicable controls while resolving a claimed exemption.

## Conflicts

When a Commission press release, FAQ and operative article differ, identify whether one is older, incomplete or refers to a different cohort. Compare dates and exact provisions. The published legal act controls; a consolidated aid can contain editorial limitations. An official source can still be a draft or summary.

For a suspected repeal, find the repealing provision and its commencement, not just a simplification announcement. For new standards, find the exact OJ citation under the particular law and its restrictions. For national transposition, verify final promulgation and section-specific commencement.

## Avoid false freshness

A fresh web crawl or new page footer is not a new legal version. Do not update `checked_on` merely because an automated fetch succeeded. A source review requires checking relevant substantive content and status. Preserve the distinction between the time bytes were retrieved and the time a qualified reviewer evaluated their legal effect.

If using hashes, normalise irrelevant chrome carefully and retain enough provenance to reconstruct the meaningful change. A changed cookie banner should not automatically open legal blockers. An unchanged page cannot prove that no amending act was published elsewhere.

## Evidence language

Use 'the current official source states', 'the accessible summary reports', 'the proposal would', and 'for this platform, the inferred impact is' accurately. Do not label every technical recommendation a legal requirement. Cite the actual source near the claim in user-facing reports and preserve stable URLs in files.
