# Primary source map

## EU sources

| Topic | Primary route | Review method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regulations/directives/amendments | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ and Official Journal | Identify CELEX/ELI, current consolidation, amendments, authentic act and application rules |
| Legislative progress | European Parliament Legislative Observatory and Council legislative records | Follow procedure to formal adoption/publication; a proposal/political agreement is not enough |
| Court interpretation | https://curia.europa.eu/ and EUR-Lex judgments | Record case, court, date, operative ruling and procedural status; distinguish AG opinion |
| Privacy | https://www.edpb.europa.eu/ and competent national authority | Separate draft/final guidance, decisions and binding law |
| AI Act | https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/ and https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/ | Prefer current operative article; verify guidance/code/template status and dates |
| Cyber/DSA/Data Act | Commission digital-strategy pages, ENISA, national competent bodies | Use overview for discovery and practical routes, then exact legal scope/transition |
| Harmonised standards | Commission/OJ citations and official standards publisher | Distinguish publication, requested revision and actual citation under a specific act |
| VAT and ViDA | https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/ plus operative EU/national text | Separate adopted legislation, implementation details and staged application |
| Adequacy/transfers | Commission adequacy decisions, EDPB and relevant certification register | Verify current validity, recipient entity/scope and the transfer at issue |

## German overlay

Use https://www.recht.bund.de/ for promulgated federal law; https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ for consolidated federal texts; official Land portals for state law; and the current official treaty text/commencement for media treaties. A consolidated page can be easier to read but must be reconciled with the actual amending act when dates conflict.

Consult BfDI and the relevant Land data-protection authority/DSK for privacy; BSI for BSIG/NIS2 and cyber procedures; Bundesnetzagentur and designated sector authorities for DDG/DSA/AI duties; the relevant accessibility market-surveillance body for BFSG; BMF/BZSt and statutory sources for tax; BfJ and competent criminal-justice authorities for relevant evidence/order processes. Confirm competence for the specific matter rather than assuming one national authority handles everything.

## Contractual and technical sources

Track exact supplier product/terms/version, negotiated overrides, processing annexes, model licences, support policies and published change notices. Treat a vendor marketing page differently from an executed contract. Track official specification/library changes where they affect promised or legally required behaviour.

Use commentary only to identify possible issues, terms or cases to inspect. Preserve the primary evidence behind any conclusion. Do not send confidential platform or personal details to public search queries when an abstract query will resolve the law.
