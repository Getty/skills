# Legal status and time

## Status is independent of publisher

An enacted law can have future provisions. A directive can require national transposition whose effective text varies. A final regulator guideline is still different from a statute. A voluntary code can have an assessed compliance role without becoming a blanket mandatory implementation. A technical standard can be published without its citation creating a presumption under the relevant legal act.

For case law, distinguish a final judgment, interim order, first-instance ruling, pending appeal, preliminary reference and Advocate General opinion. Record the legal issue and relevant facts; do not universalise a case headline.

## Date fields

Maintain publication date, entry into force, general application date, provision-specific application, national implementation, transition deadline, legacy-system population, source verification date and internal review due date. Do not combine them in `effective_date` without explaining what it means.

For the AI Act, a model placed before 2 August 2025, a system placed before 2 August 2026 and a new system launched in November 2026 can have different transitional treatment. A model's date does not automatically confer its transition on a newly assembled system.

For consumer rights, contract date, service performance, information delivery, express requests/acknowledgements and withdrawal period can matter. For CRA, manufacturer reporting dates differ from the main product requirements and role-specific regimes. For standards, identify the cited edition, legal act and restrictions.

## Time calculation

Calendar review dates in the helper are not statutory incident deadlines. Actual incident clocks can run in hours from awareness; store timestamps/timezone and the precise triggering fact. Other deadlines can depend on delivery, working days, national holiday rules or procedural law. Use a verified legal calculation for the actual case.

User-facing schedules for a German operation normally use Europe/Berlin with daylight-saving handling. Preserve machine timestamps in a consistent format plus their source. Never silently convert '72 hours' into 'three business days'.

## Proposals and preparation

Create a watch item and optional low-cost preparation for a plausible proposal. Do not remove existing controls or claim a legal exemption until the enacted law, relevant scope and commencement are verified. Conversely, do not keep calling a reform a proposal once its operative act is published. The 2026 AI Omnibus and broader data/GDPR proposals must be tracked separately.

Mark internal targets as internal. An organisation can choose earlier readiness without asserting that the law mandates the earlier date. Capture the exact basis when a customer contract imposes a stricter schedule.
