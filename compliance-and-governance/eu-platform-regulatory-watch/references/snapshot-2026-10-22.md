## Other monitored transitions

The specialist skills identify German NIS2/BSIG, German KI-MIG, e-evidence, Data Act switching fees, German e-invoicing and ViDA transitions. Use their exact sources and conditional implementation rules. Do not turn their dates into a universal deadline for every AI platform.

Do not infer an enacted GDPR/RoPA exemption or P2B repeal from the fact that the AI Omnibus was enacted. Check each separate legislative instrument and operative amendment. Likewise, do not describe an adopted measure as 'only proposed' because an old policy overview has not been updated.

## Retrieval scope

The AI dates were cross-checked against the official consolidated article reproductions and official timeline. EUR-Lex original direct fetches were blocked. BGB §356a, the CRA overview, EDPB consultation and AccessibleEU announcement were directly readable. The older publication date of an official announcement limits what it proves about later status.

## Preparation priorities

For a relevant product, examine near-term December 2026 AI transitions and January 2027 switching/invoicing changes early enough to implement them. Record the role/system/contract cohort. Maintain existing GDPR, customer rights and other applicable duties while planning future ones. Review sources again before actual launch or reliance on an exemption.
