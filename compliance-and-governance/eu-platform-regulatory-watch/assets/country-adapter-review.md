# Country adapter review

Record target country, legal entities, customer types and targeted markets. Verify national privacy opening clauses, terminal access/marketing, consumer rights, accessibility, NIS2, AI Act authority allocation, media/youth, employment, copyright, tax/invoice and relevant sector rules. For each, record official source, current version, implementation/commencement, applicability, difference from the German adapter, required control and review owner.

Do not copy German thresholds or procedures by translating their names. Mark missing national research explicitly and contain only the decisions that rely on it.
