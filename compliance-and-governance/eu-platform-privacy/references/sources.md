## Refresh rules

For each assessment, record `retrieved_at`, source version/publication date, legal status, applicable date, proposition supported, and uncertainty. A retrieval date is not the date a law became applicable.

Resolve apparent conflicts in this order: applicable law and amendments; relevant judgments; competent-authority decisions; adopted supervisory guidance; official explanatory material. A later proposal does not displace law. A new judgment may change interpretation without changing the statutory text.

Before relying on a numerical exemption, obtain the operative rule and check all exceptions. If the amended Art 30 status cannot be established, keep the processing inventory as an operational control and label the exemption decision unresolved. Do not claim that every small business is exempt or that keeping a voluntary record is legally mandatory in every case.

Refresh for a new country, model/data purpose, sensitive-data feature, disputed rights response, material incident, legal amendment, or authority decision. A scheduled review frequency chosen by the operator is an organisational control, not a legal deadline.
