# Digital-service lifecycle

## Contract conformity

Translate advertised and agreed qualities into service acceptance criteria: model access, language coverage, file limits, latency commitments, storage, supported browsers, export and interoperability. Under the digital-product regime, express terms are not the only relevant expectations. Do not treat an AI-error disclaimer as permission to deliver a wholly unsuitable service. If relying on a deviation from objective requirements in Germany, assess the specific, separate agreement requirements of § 327h; a broad buried exclusion is not a substitute. [C12]

Create a promise-to-control map: landing-page claim → contract clause → implementation → measurable test → owner. Route exaggerated “100% accurate,” “fully GDPR compliant,” “all outputs are copyright free,” or “guaranteed confidential” claims to the appropriate specialist and remove unsupported claims before publication.

## Updates and support

For applicable digital products, § 327f requires necessary updates, including security updates, for the legally relevant period. Continuous supply and one-off supply use different duration rules. Maintain a support policy with version coverage, notification route, deployment responsibility and end-of-support decision record. Customer failure to install is not a general escape: the statutory conditions and quality of installation instructions matter. [C13]

Tie upstream dependency end-of-life to a consumer-impact assessment. A provider discontinuing an API can create a remediation need; it does not automatically extinguish the platform's contractual duties. Keep fallbacks, export and refund/remedy pathways operational during service retirement.

## Changes beyond maintenance

For ongoing German digital services, assess § 327r for changes beyond maintaining conformity. The contract needs the required change basis and valid reason; the change must not add costs under that provision, and information duties apply. Where access or use is impaired beyond the minor threshold, timely durable-medium notice and statutory termination rights can matter. Do not apply this rule as universal authority for unilateral price increases. Price changes require their own validity analysis. [C14]

For every material model switch record old/new capabilities, affected promises, cohort, reasons, migration options, notice text and remedy. A feature flag that changes paid functionality is a legal change trigger even if no contract file changed. Measure capability regressions on representative tasks before selecting the contractual path.

## Terms and renewal

Review unfair terms under BGB §§ 305–310, especially § 307 and relevant § 309 rules. For the covered recurring-service contracts, § 309 no. 9 restricts initial binding terms, automatic renewal and notice terms; do not apply it mechanically without contract type and cohort dates. Model renewals from the legally reviewed rule set rather than the payment provider's default subscription option. [C15]

Do not silently accept an upstream provider's right to delete all stored data, suspend without appeal or materially change processing while promising consumers the opposite. Coordinate vendor and consumer obligations. Separate contractual suspension for misuse, security containment, mandatory rights and final deletion.

## Remedies workflow

Record alleged non-supply or defect, reproducibility, contractual promises, relevant device/environment and available evidence. Offer the legally appropriate cure, reduction, termination or refund path under the applicable national implementation, preserving deadlines and communications. Distinguish a defect from a subjective dislike without treating probabilistic AI behavior as exempt from conformity rules.

Require a legal owner for contested policy changes and recurring patterns of denied remedies. Feed systemic complaints into release review; do not allow a passing unit test to close a customer-rights incident.

## Post-termination content and privacy choices

Assess BGB § 327p for continued use and provision of qualifying non-personal content the consumer supplied or created. Its exceptions and on-request, free, machine-readable provision rules are distinct from GDPR access/portability and Data Act customer switching. Do not immediately delete every useful export when terminating access, or assume non-personal content can always be reused for training after termination. [C18]

Under § 327q, exercising privacy rights does not itself invalidate the contract. A provider's possible termination after consent withdrawal or objection has specific conditions and a balancing test; damages for the reduction in permitted data processing are excluded. Do not automate account punishment for opting out. Coordinate the actual processing basis, necessary service operation and contract consequences with privacy and legal owners. [C19]
