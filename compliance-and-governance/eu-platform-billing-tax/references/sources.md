## Source and rule maintenance

Record retrieval success rather than a generic “verified” stamp. Check current authority guidance where the statutory text delegates format or administrative detail. Freeze the selected legal/tax policy version for each transaction, retain later corrections and review new laws by effective date. Do not silently replace missing primary evidence with an unsourced blog or a model's recollection.
