# Metering and invoices

## Define the billable event

Specify event ID, tenant, contract/price version, service time, model/tool, quantity, unit, currency and charge treatment. Decide how cached inputs, generated outputs, failed calls, client cancellations, retries, background agents and third-party tools are charged. Distinguish provider cost from customer charge: they can use different units and multipliers. Keep an auditable mapping.

Use immutable usage events with deduplication, sequence/reconciliation controls and correction events. A streamed response reconnect must not double-bill. A delayed event must retain its actual service period and the approved rule for late billing. Do not log full prompts merely to substantiate token counts; retain the minimum necessary usage evidence and controlled references for disputes.

## Calculate deterministically

Use decimal or integer minor-unit arithmetic, explicit rounding and versioned rates. Test zero usage, negative adjustments, refunds, currencies with different minor units, tax-inclusive pricing, discounts and bundled supplies. Do not feed a free-form LLM output directly into the ledger. A model may assist classification research, but approved deterministic policy controls amounts.

## Invoice contents

For Germany, map the applicable UStG § 14/14a requirements and exceptions to structured fields: correct parties, issuer identification, issue date, uniquely allocated invoice number, supplied service/period, consideration, applicable rate/tax or exemption annotation and special statements where required. Verify exact fields for the transaction type rather than applying a universal template. [T6]

Include an understandable service description and period; “AI tokens” alone may be poor evidence of the service. Reference a detailed usage statement where appropriate without embedding private content. Keep seller identity and bank details under controlled change management. An invoice generated by a PSP on behalf of the seller still needs accurate responsibility and data.

## Corrections

Preserve issued records. Create a linked correction/cancellation and new document where required, with reason, original reference and authorized review. Distinguish a commercial credit note from statutory self-billing terminology. Do not silently modify a stored PDF while leaving XML and ledger values unchanged. Ensure the customer receives the valid corrected document through the approved process.

## Payment reconciliation

Track invoice, authorization, capture, settlement, fees, refunds, chargebacks and ledger entry separately. Webhooks can arrive more than once, out of order or after a timeout; verify signatures, reject replay, use idempotent handlers and reconcile against PSP records. A successful browser redirect is not payment evidence. A payment failure does not by itself decide whether a contract formed.

## Acceptance evidence

Demonstrate one request charged once across retries, exact period/rate version reconstruction, consistent invoice/PDF/XML totals, correction traceability and reconciliation to payment/ledger. Ask finance to validate meaningful sample transactions, including cross-border and refunded cases. Record the scope and approved policy version.
