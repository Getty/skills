# Business contract review

- Deal, entities, customer purpose and signing authority:
- Governing law, countries and regulated-use screening:
- Actual service/deployment/model description:
- Contract documents, versions and precedence:
- Personal-data and AI-system roles:
- Upstream/downstream gaps:
- Clause-to-control register:
- SLA measurement and incident communication evidence:
- Data Act applicability, current-law verification and exception analysis:
- Exit/export/retrieval/deletion rehearsal:
- Liability, IP, secrecy and unsupported claims:
- Country-specific issues:
- Proposed redlines and implementation costs:
- Outstanding legal/commercial decisions:
- Authorized signer / release owner / renewal owner:

## Issue record

`Clause | obligation | actual behavior | evidence | legal/commercial/technical issue | impact | options | preferred option and reason | approver | due date`

State whether evidence is a vendor assertion, a signed commitment or an observed control. Do not treat a contract signature as a completed security implementation.
