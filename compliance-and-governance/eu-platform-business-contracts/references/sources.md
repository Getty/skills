## Freshness gates

For Data Act exceptions or timing, compare the consolidated regulation, amendment instruments and application dates. Do not treat a Commission proposal as current law. For standards and certifications, verify scope and edition. For private terms, retrieve the version applicable to the exact product and account, not just a public landing page.

Record inaccessible sources as pending verification. Continue technical preparation, but do not issue definitive contractual advice for a disputed clause without resolving the source gap. Future legal changes should create an assessment ticket, not silently rewrite accepted customer contracts.
