# Clauses and risk allocation

## Service scope and AI limitations

Define supported use, interfaces, model availability, versions, tools, capacity and human support. Distinguish accuracy evaluation from contractual warranties. Explain foreseeable output limitations precisely without suggesting all service failures are excused. Keep a path for correcting defective integrations and for escalating high-impact customer uses to AI Act and sector specialists.

For warranties such as “no training,” “data never leaves the EU,” “all outputs belong exclusively to you,” and “zero retention,” demand an end-to-end implementation and rights analysis. A promise can be false because of fallback models, abuse logs, remote support, customer connectors or supplier license restrictions. Use narrower substantiated wording where necessary; legal/commercial owners decide the contract.

## Liability and remedies

Map liability caps, exclusions, indemnities, claim notification, defense control and insurance to the actual risk. Assess mandatory limits under governing law. In Germany, do not presume broad US-style exclusions for all damages survive BGB standard-terms review; intentional conduct and relevant statutory protection need specific treatment. Identify whether a cap is per claim, per year or aggregate and what happens after termination. [B1, B3]

Do not claim service credits automatically replace every statutory or contractual remedy. Distinguish credit entitlement from refund, damages, cure, termination and regulatory liability. A customer's indemnity for uploaded content does not itself create rights to use that content or displace the platform's own legal obligations.

## Data and IP clauses

Separate customer inputs, third-party data, outputs, usage metadata, model weights, custom integrations and platform software. Specify the rights needed to perform the service and any optional training or product-improvement use. Check that upstream terms permit the downstream licenses offered. Copyright, database rights, trade secrets and personal data are different legal categories; a blanket ownership sentence cannot solve all of them.

For confidential customer materials, record who can access them, why, where and under which safeguards. Under Germany's GeschGehG, reasonable confidentiality measures matter to trade-secret protection; implement contractual restrictions alongside access control, vendor duties and handling procedures. Do not claim that marking a file “confidential” is sufficient by itself. [B4]

## Suspension and change

Define objectively reviewable triggers for abuse, nonpayment, security containment and legal necessity. Preserve evidence, use proportionate measures, support review and restoration, and maintain required export/access rights. Emergency security action should not become an unbounded permission to delete tenant data. Keep customer notification rules synchronized with incident duties.

For price, model or feature changes, identify authority, notice period, approval and exit rights. Configure tenant cohorts by the agreement that actually applies. Avoid a global upgrade that violates a customer's fixed-price, residency or model-version commitment. Prepare clause alternatives and a migration plan before asking customers to accept changes.

## Negotiation packet

For each issue include current text, proposed text, legal basis or commercial reason, affected controls, implementation cost, risk if unchanged, accountable approver and expiry/review date. Never silently resolve a conflict by selecting the weaker document. Use a tracked redline if the document format supports it.
