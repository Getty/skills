# Assurance limits and review quality

## Levels of evidence

A policy states intention. Code/configuration indicates a possible implementation. A test demonstrates a property under its conditions. Production observation adds operational evidence for a time and population. An authorised specialist decision interprets the applicable duty. None alone replaces all others.

Report the strongest supported statement. 'The consent test showed no requests to the selected analytics host after refusal in this browser/build' is narrower and more useful than 'tracking is GDPR compliant'. A single automated accessibility scan cannot establish the entire accessible user journey. A DPA does not establish how the vendor actually stores data.

## Useful independence

For material controls, have a reviewer or separate agent assess raw evidence with a task-focused brief, without supplying the desired conclusion. Use realistic scenarios and actual diffs. Do not leak expected answers and then treat agreement as validation. Preserve contrary findings and resolve them against evidence.

Independent review is especially useful for hidden egress, complex deletion, legal classification, source interpretation, prompt-injection boundaries and changes to the gate itself. Routine reversible formatting changes do not need an elaborate review exercise.

## Handling uncertainty

Differentiate unknown facts, disputed interpretation, inaccessible sources, expired evidence and a confirmed defect. Define the next action that resolves each. Block or contain the affected decision when proceeding relies on an unresolved material premise; continue unrelated authorised work.

Do not inflate small uncertainties into a declaration that nothing can proceed. Do not erase uncertainty by adding a generic disclaimer or lowering a threshold. A reviewer must receive concrete alternatives, consequences and evidence.

## Never certify through a score

Avoid a single numerical compliance percentage: obligations do not have interchangeable legal weight, scope can be wrong and some critical failures dominate. Prefer a scoped register of applicable duties, verified controls, gaps and deadlines. A successful local checker only confirms its declared consistency constraints.
