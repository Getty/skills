# Sources and normative status

This skill is primarily an engineering workflow. Its exact check formats, hashes, validity windows, CI design and recommended tests are internal assurance methods, not legal forms prescribed for all platforms.

Relevant legal anchors include GDPR Articles 5(2), 24, 25 and 32 for accountability/design/security; applicable AI Act Articles 9-17, 26, 72 and 73 for relevant high-risk roles; AI Act transparency/GPAI provisions; and any applicable NIS2/national cyber, CRA, consumer or contractual duty. Apply their exact current scope and dates through the specialist skills before turning them into mandatory controls.

Primary sources: [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [current AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng), [official AI Act Explorer](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act-explorer), [NIS2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj/eng), [CRA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng). For national sources and current legal status, use the corresponding specialist registry.

Research snapshot is 2026-10-22. Do not repeat old high-risk AI application dates, use a proposed omnibus reform as enacted GDPR law, or cite a voluntary standard as mandatory without verifying its actual legal role. Store source and evidence review dates separately.
