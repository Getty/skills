# Information, remediation and reporting

## Public information

For German in-scope services, assess BFSG § 14 and Annex 3. Required information describes the service, applicable accessibility requirements, relevant operation and how the requirements are met, and identifies the competent market-surveillance authority. Make it accessible and maintain it while the service is offered. The statute permits the specified information in terms or another clearly perceptible form; do not blindly copy a public-sector declaration template and assume it meets private-sector requirements. [A13, A14]

Prepare information from real evidence. State the evaluated scope and known limitations accurately. Do not claim every generated PDF or every user upload is conformant merely because the application shell passed a check. Provide a useful contact route and explain how users can report a barrier without having to diagnose the standard criterion.

## Handle a barrier report

Capture the affected task, environment, assistive technology if voluntarily supplied, impact and reproduction details. Do not require medical proof to report a usability barrier. Reproduce with synthetic data, assign an owner and offer a workable interim route where appropriate. Preserve the original report and avoid repeatedly asking the user for the same inaccessible action.

Triage by inability to complete essential actions, loss of autonomy, financial/legal consequences, safety and number of affected users. A broken cancellation or MFA route can require urgent work even if it affects a small group. Route statutory deadlines separately from an internal repair SLA.

## German nonconformity duties

BFSG § 14 includes corrective and notification duties for service nonconformity. Do not assume the authority-notification duty applies only after an authority contacts the business. The responsible legal/compliance owner should assess the facts, affected member states and required communication promptly. Prepare precise facts, scope, measures and evidence; execute notices only under authorization. [A13]

For a disproportionate-burden claim, use the statutory assessment, documentation and review requirements rather than an engineering guess. Record cost evidence, benefits, service category, funding and alternatives, then obtain the accountable legal/business decision. A public promise to fix everything eventually is not a completed statutory assessment. [A6]

## Evidence and maintenance

Link public information to the internal requirement matrix and evaluation date. Recheck after changed authentication, checkout, design system, AI rendering or standard citation. Track repeat reports and regression rate. Do not silently remove a known limitation from the public information until the repair is verified and the relevant scope is understood.

Keep accessible communication templates in the platform's normal document workflow, not in an inaccessible scanned attachment. Coordinate accessibility with consumer rights and privacy so that a customer can exercise rights without being forced through a barrier.
