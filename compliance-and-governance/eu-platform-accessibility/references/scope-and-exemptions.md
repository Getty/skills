# Scope and exemptions

## EU core

The European Accessibility Act is Directive (EU) 2019/882. Determine whether the product or consumer service falls within its listed categories and inspect the national implementation for each relevant market. Ecommerce is a key route for AI subscriptions; there is no reliable rule that every website is in scope or that every AI tool is exempt. Public-sector website/app obligations under Directive (EU) 2016/2102 are a separate branch. [A1, A2]

Classify the complete service and the purchase interface separately where necessary. Identify consumer use, provision through websites/apps, and the transaction the user is trying to complete. Pure B2B software does not become exempt from all accessibility obligations: public procurement, contract requirements and other law may still apply. Conversely, a general marketing page is not automatically a covered ecommerce service solely because it exists online.

## German adapter

Assess BFSG § 1 and § 2 definitions. For an in-scope service, map BFSGV general requirements and service-specific requirements. BFSGV § 19 expressly addresses identification, authentication, security and payment functions in ecommerce. Do not narrow the engineering review to a product catalog while excluding the login or payment component that prevents purchase. [A3, A4]

BFSG § 3(3) exempts microenterprises providing services from the specified accessibility duty. Section 2 no. 17 defines the relevant scale as fewer than ten persons and either annual turnover or annual balance-sheet total not exceeding €2 million. Verify the facts and applicable enterprise/counting rules; do not use headcount alone. Do not apply this service exemption indiscriminately to businesses placing covered products on the market. [A5]

## Limited exceptions and transitions

Assess fundamental alteration and disproportionate burden per requirement and service, with the statutory criteria and evidence. Lack of planning or a vendor's inconvenience is not sufficient proof. Under BFSG § 17, documentation, reassessment, funding limitations and notification duties can apply; record the legal basis and accountable decision. Preserve the requirements that remain feasible. [A6]

BFSG § 38 has specific transitional rules for certain existing products used to deliver services and pre-existing service contracts, with the relevant outer date of 2030-06-27. It is not a blanket five-year grace period for every existing website. Identify the original contract, whether it remains unchanged and the exact statutory branch. [A7]

## Country adapter fields

Record national statute and enforcement authority; covered service; entity size and calculation method; dates and transitions; information/statement duties; notification and complaint routes; applicable standards/citations; language; public-sector and procurement overlays. Mark the adapter `incomplete` where legal verification is missing. Reusing the English technical workflow is appropriate; reusing German legal conclusions without checking the country's rules is not.

## Evidence

The applicability decision must be traceable to business facts and current law. A product manager's label “B2B” or “small company” is an assertion requiring verification. Keep approved exemptions in a register with scope, evidence, owner and review trigger. Continue accessible engineering as a product decision if desired, but identify it as policy rather than inventing a legal duty.
