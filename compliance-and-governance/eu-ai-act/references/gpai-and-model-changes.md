# GPAI and model changes

## Determine whether the organisation is a model provider

Use Article 3 definitions and current Commission GPAI guidelines. Record model capabilities, development/modification, responsible entity, distribution and market placement. Calling an API does not in itself make the caller the provider of the underlying model. Fine-tuning does not automatically impose all GPAI duties, but material modifications, release under a new name and capability changes need their own assessment. Do not use an arbitrary parameter-count rule.

Differentiate statutory criteria from Commission guidance heuristics about training compute and model modification. Obtain actual technical facts before applying those heuristics and check for updates. A system modification assessment under Article 25 is not the same test as the GPAI modification analysis.

## Article 53 obligations

Where applicable, maintain technical documentation for competent authorities, information enabling downstream providers to understand capabilities and limitations, a policy for compliance with Union copyright including rights reservations, and the public training-content summary using the AI Office template. Track model lineage, datasets, release dates and changes. The IP skill covers rights clearance; a summary is not a licence.

The specific Article 53(2) open-source exception concerns some documentation/information duties and requires the actual licence and disclosure conditions. It does not remove copyright-policy or training-summary obligations. It does not apply in the same way to systemic-risk models. 'Open weights', a model card or a permissive code licence alone is insufficient.

Assess Article 54 authorised-representative requirements for non-EU providers and the exact exceptions. A reseller's European address is not proof of legal representation.

## Systemic risk

Articles 51-52 use high-impact capability criteria/designation and a compute-based presumption. The current Article 51 contains the 10^25-FLOP training threshold; verify amended/delegated rules and the possibility of designation beyond that threshold. Do not say 'below threshold means no systemic risk'. Route threshold proximity or designation to specialised assessment.

For a provider of a GPAI model with systemic risk, assess Article 55 evaluation/adversarial testing, systemic-risk mitigation, serious-incident tracking/reporting and cybersecurity. Do not reuse the high-risk-system Article 73 deadline as a universal GPAI incident clock.

## Model onboarding packet

Record provider entity, model/version, release date, licence and restrictions, data-processing terms, retention/training settings, downstream documentation, safety/evaluation evidence, content marking support, change notifications and end-of-life policy. Check service routing, fallbacks and customer agreements against the actual vendor. A provider's claim of compliance does not eliminate the platform's own duties.

## Change control

Reevaluate model substitution, fine-tuning, distillation, continued pretraining, merged models, quantisation, modality changes and new tool capabilities. Their legal effects differ; a quantisation need not create the same duties as a newly capable model, but it can change performance and marking behaviour. Freeze a version where contractually and technically possible, test updates and define rollback.

The GPAI Code of Practice is voluntary. Record whether it is an adopted compliance route, what it covers and what evidence remains necessary. Do not assume membership, signature or regulator acceptance.

Sources: Articles 3, 51-56 and 111(3); Commission GPAI guidelines, FAQ and training-summary template in sources.md. GPAI duties generally apply from 2 August 2025, with Article 111(3)'s 2 August 2027 deadline for models placed on the market before 2 August 2025.
