# Dates, amendments and transition cohorts

Verified snapshot: 2026-10-22. The AI Omnibus is enacted in Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 and entered into force on 27 July 2026. It is not merely the November 2025 proposal or May 2026 political agreement. Use current consolidated Regulation 2024/1689 and the amending act.

## Legacy systems

Article 111(2) addresses specified high-risk systems placed/put into service before the relevant Chapter III application date, with significant design changes and public-authority system deadlines. Record each cohort and change; do not use a global 'grandfathered' flag. Article 111(1) has a separate large-scale IT-system rule. These transitions do not erase applicable prohibitions, GDPR, consumer law or other independent duties.

## Source conflicts

The official AI Act Explorer declares a consolidated 27 July 2026 basis. Its page summaries can simplify details, and a page or FAQ may lag. For example, inspect exact Articles 6/49/71 for changes to registration rather than relying on a press release about reduced administration. Use operative text over summary. Do not silently choose a less demanding date.

EUR-Lex full-text opens were blocked during this research. The amendment's official indexed record, official Commission announcement and official consolidated article reproductions independently support the enacted status/timeline; they do not justify claiming every line of the original gazette was directly read. For a decisive disputed amendment, retrieve the authenticated act before final legal sign-off.
