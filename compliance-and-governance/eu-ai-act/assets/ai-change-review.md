# AI change review

1. Identify old/new system, model, prompt, tool, data, purpose and deployment population.
2. Explain role/classification changes and whether a legal substantial modification may occur.
3. Refresh the affected obligations and the exact transitional cohort.
4. Verify upstream documentation, terms, content marking and data-routing changes.
5. Define realistic acceptance cases and negative/adversarial cases.
6. Record tests, dataset rights, performance limits and rollback.
7. Update user/deployer instructions and literacy materials where affected.
8. Present unresolved legal decisions and any authority submission for authorised review.

Do not approve a model switch solely because its provider claims better benchmarks.
