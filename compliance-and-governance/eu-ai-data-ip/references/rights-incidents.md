# Rights complaints and remediation

## Intake

Record claimant/contact in a restricted system, claimed right, affected asset/output/model, URLs/IDs, basis/evidence, requested action, awareness date and urgency. Authenticate claims proportionately; do not demand unnecessary identification or accept an unsupported assertion as conclusive. Distinguish copyright notice, personal-data request, confidential-data exposure, trademark complaint and contractual licence issue.

## Identify obligations

If the service is a DSA hosting/intermediary service for the content at issue, apply the relevant notice/action, statement-of-reasons, appeal and reporting duties through `eu-platform-content-safety`. Own generated/editorial content needs its own analysis. GDPR erasure and breach workflows are separate. Record applicable clocks and owners rather than assigning a universal takedown deadline.

## Containment and evidence

Restrict the specific asset/use or public access where justified within authorised processes. Preserve minimal necessary evidence and legal holds under restricted access. Avoid wholesale deletion that destroys incident facts or disproportional account suspension without the appropriate process. Do not forward disputed material to unauthorised persons.

## Trace downstream

Use the provenance graph to identify copies, chunks, embeddings, caches, public shares, exported files, training/evaluation snapshots and model versions. Evaluate contractual notifications and sublicences. A removed ingestion URL does not establish that the corpus or model no longer contains the material.

For model remediation, describe what is technically and legally required and what is uncertain. Exclusion from future training, corpus deletion, retrieval blocking, output restrictions, model withdrawal, retraining and tested unlearning are different actions. Do not report an unverified unlearning result as complete removal.

## Closure

Keep the rationale, sources, affected releases, actions, tests, communication approval, submissions/acknowledgements and review rights as applicable. Prepare responses without sending them unless authorised. Do not admit liability or settle a licence dispute on the organisation's behalf without authority.

Reopen if new evidence, a court order, revised rights information or a renewed system export changes the decision. Feed valid incidents into ingestion controls and licence/provenance review without copying customer confidential content into unrestricted training or tickets.
