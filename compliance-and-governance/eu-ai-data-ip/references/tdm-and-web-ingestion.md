# TDM and web ingestion

## EU and German framework

DSM Directive Article 3 provides a specialised TDM route for qualifying research organisations/cultural heritage institutions and scientific research, with conditions. Article 4 provides a separate general exception with lawful-access and rights-reservation conditions. Examine national implementation and current case law for the specific operation. Do not resolve every generative-training dispute by asserting that all training is either always permitted or always prohibited.

Germany's UrhG §44b defines TDM, permits specified reproductions of lawfully accessible works, requires deletion when no longer necessary for that TDM, and makes the permission conditional on absence of an effective reservation; for online works the reservation must be machine-readable. UrhG §60d has distinct eligibility, purpose, sharing, security and retention conditions. A commercial product team is not automatically a qualifying research organisation, and private influence/preferential-access conditions matter. Database rights require their own analysis, including §87c.

## Acquisition workflow

1. Identify the exact source and intended operation. Keep access/copying separate from later retrieval/publication.
2. Confirm lawful access without bypassing authentication, technical restrictions or paid access conditions.
3. Inspect relevant current terms, licences and machine-readable rights reservations. Preserve the observation time and source version.
4. Decide whether a licence or a specific statutory exception supports the proposed act. Route unsettled interpretation to a qualified reviewer.
5. Apply collection limits, purpose controls, security and deletion obligations.
6. Preserve necessary provenance and review the outputs/downstream use independently.

Do not claim `robots.txt` alone clears copyright or that every possible machine-readable reservation is equivalent. Record the mechanisms actually encountered and assess their significance. Do not infer a contractual licence merely from the absence of a reservation. Rate limits and service protection remain operational/access constraints even where a copying exception may be available.

## RAG is not automatically one legal operation

Copying documents into a persistent answer corpus, serving snippets and allowing exports raises questions beyond an analytical mining phase. Evaluate the exact retrieval and output use. A summary can still reproduce protected expression or expose confidential content; a citation does not clear that use.

## Legal developments

Training/TDM disputes, machine-readable reservations and model memorisation can involve evolving national and EU case law. Verify court, date, decision, procedural status and scope. A first-instance decision, pending reference, Advocate General opinion or Parliament resolution is not automatically settled EU-wide law. The 2026 Parliament copyright/AI resolution is a policy item unless and until enacted measures change the operative law.
